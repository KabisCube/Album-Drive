Bundled as the rounded CJK companion to Album Round. This full TTF contains
44,806 Unicode characters, including kana, modern Hangul syllables, and common
Simplified/Traditional Chinese characters. Its default Han forms follow the
Simplified Chinese variant. It does not cover every historical or rare character.

Album Round's original Latin font remains separately licensed; this companion
font retains its original family name, authorship, and SIL Open Font License.
